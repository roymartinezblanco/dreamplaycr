About DreamPlay

Our Mission

We are committed to a better world where science and technology are accessible to everyone, and level the playing field for people with disabilities.

DreamPlay was born in Costa Rica with a simple but ambitious belief: that immersive technology — virtual reality, interactive experiences, and digital tools — should not be a luxury. It should be a bridge. A bridge to healthcare, education, culture, and opportunity for anyone, regardless of ability or circumstance.

Over the years we have partnered with hospitals, universities, museums, NGOs, and startups to build experiences that matter. From helping Alzheimer's patients at Costa Rica's National Psychiatric Hospital to inspiring the next generation of coders at Museo de Los Niños, our work has always been grounded in the belief that technology is most powerful when it is in service of people.

A note on our current status: We have decided to take advantage of the opportunity to step back, take a break to recharge, renew and rebuild. We are grateful for everyone who has been part of this journey and look forward to what comes next.
